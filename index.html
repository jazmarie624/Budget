How your monthly amount left works

Income for selected month − bill reservations from that month’s checks − upcoming-expense reservations − actual spending − planned savings − debt payments = planning estimate.

Reservations are treated as committed, because money you set aside for Rent, Christmas, or a layaway is not truly free to spend.
